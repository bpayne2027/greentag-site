What the app does

  • Connects to a Bluetooth pressure gauge and reads pressure live
  • Times the test and tracks pressure from start to finish
  • Shows a clear pass or fail result when the test ends
  • Gives you a record of the test to keep with the job

Supported gauge

Measureman / RiTHERM MA223 Bluetooth digital pressure gauge.

More gauges may be added in future updates. If you use a different Bluetooth gauge, email us and let us know which one.

Common questions

The app can't find my gauge

Make sure Bluetooth is on and the gauge is powered on and close by. Allow Bluetooth access for Green Tag in Settings → Privacy & Security → Bluetooth. Close the gauge maker's own app if it's open, since it can hold the connection.

Does it work on iPad?

Yes. Green Tag runs on iPhone and iPad.

Does the app replace the code or my inspector?

No. Green Tag helps you run and document a test. Always follow your local code and your authority having jurisdiction for the required test pressure and duration.
