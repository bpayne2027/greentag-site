What the app does

  • Connects to a Bluetooth pressure gauge and reads pressure live
  • Waits for the pressure to settle, then times the test
  • Shows a clear pass or fail result when the test ends
  • Saves a PDF record you can share to email, text, or your job software

Which gauge works with Green Tag?

The MEOKON MD-S281 Bluetooth digital pressure gauge, sold as the Measureman MA223. Turn the gauge on, open Green Tag, and tap Scan for gauge.

More gauges may be added in future updates. If you use a different Bluetooth gauge, email us and let us know which one.

Common questions

Can I try it without a gauge?

Yes. On the first screen, under "No gauge? Try a demo," tap Demo: passing test or Demo: leak. A simulated gauge runs a full test in about a minute. Demo records are stamped DEMO and aren't saved.

Why didn't the timer start right away?

Pumping heats the air in the line, and the pressure drops as it cools. Green Tag waits until the pressure holds steady, usually about a minute. If it was already flat, that minute counts toward the test.

The gauge won't connect

Make sure Bluetooth is on and Green Tag is allowed to use it: iPhone Settings, then Green Tag, then Bluetooth. Turn the gauge off and on, then scan again. Only one phone can be connected to the gauge at a time.

Where are my records?

Tap the clock icon at the top right of the app. Records are stored on your device only. Share a PDF from there to email, text, or your job software.

Does it work on iPad?

Does the app replace the code or my inspector?

No. Green Tag helps you run and document a test. Always follow your local code and your authority having jurisdiction for the required test pressure and duration.
