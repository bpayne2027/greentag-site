Green Tag privacy policy

Green Tag doesn't collect any data. Your tests stay on your phone until you choose to share them.

Effective November 23, 2026 · Green Tag: Gas Pressure Test, by Brandon Payne

This policy covers the Green Tag app for iPhone and iPad. Green Tag has no accounts, no sign-in, no ads, no analytics, and no tracking. It does not connect to the internet, and it has no servers that could receive your information.

What the app uses on your device

You can turn Bluetooth, camera, and photo access on or off anytime in iPhone Settings under Green Tag.

When you share a record

When you tap Share or Save, the app hands the PDF or image to the app or person you pick, such as email, Messages, Files, or your job software. From there, that service's own privacy policy applies. Green Tag never sends anything on its own.

What we don't do

  • We don't collect, store, sell, or share personal data.
  • We don't use advertising, analytics, or third-party tracking tools.
  • We don't track you across other apps or websites.
  • We don't read your location.

Deleting your data

Everything Green Tag stores is on your device. Deleting the app removes all of its records and gauge checks. Records you already shared stay wherever you sent them.

Children

Green Tag is a professional tool for the trades and isn't directed at children. It collects no information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date. If the app ever starts collecting data, this page and the App Store privacy label will say so before that version is released.
